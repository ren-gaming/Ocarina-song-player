Ocarina Song Player

Pitch helper for the Ocarina of Time remake — plays each song's notes through your speakers.

How to use
  1. Turn your phone/PC volume up and hold its microphone a few inches from the Switch 2 mic.
  2. Press a song below — the page plays the six notes at the exact pitch.
  3. If a song isn't recognized, use the tuner row to test each note alone and tweak the frequency.
